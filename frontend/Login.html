Welcome back.

A space for your mind.

MoodPulse processes profile information, heart-rate readings, journal entries, camera images for emotion analysis, and the resulting emotion labels to provide your account, wellness summaries, and advice. When you request advice, selected journal text and derived data may be sent to the language-model service configured for this installation; that service may be hosted locally or by a third party. This is a wellness tool, not a medical service.

Your account and profile are stored securely. Passwords are protected with Argon2id before they are stored.
